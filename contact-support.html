    QUICK ANSWERS

    Before you send a message

    A few common answers can save you a wait.

    How do I reset my password?

    Open the login page, choose “Forgot Password,” and follow the Firebase email reset flow.

    How do I get useful progress?

    Log activities, sleep, and nutrition consistently. FitFlow uses those records to surface trends and milestones.

    How is my account protected?

    Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication, while user records are protected by the project's Firebase data rules.

    Something is not working

    Tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. Include the page name and any visible error message.
