    FITFLOW NOTES

    How the product works

    Short notes on the ideas behind FitFlow. These are intentionally practical rather than pretending to be a health publication.

    Product · The loop

    Why FitFlow uses Log → Understand → Act → Review

    A tracker is only useful if the information changes what you do. FitFlow keeps the loop simple: record a few useful signals, inspect the history, choose a realistic next step, then return to review what changed.

    Product · Progress

    Why consistency matters more than a perfect day

    One workout or one meal says very little. Repeated records make trends visible, which is why FitFlow's progress views focus on days and weeks rather than a single score.

    Engineering · Trust

    Keeping recommendations explainable

    The dashboard's daily plan is based on recent records and simple rules. That keeps the recommendation understandable and avoids presenting a generative model as something the product does not actually provide.
