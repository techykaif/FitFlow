The idea behind FitFlow

A wellness tracker designed around the next decision.

FitFlow is a personal wellness companion built to turn activity, nutrition and sleep records into a simple loop: Log → Understand → Act → Review.

Why it exists

Not another place to enter numbers

Capture useful data

Record the parts of daily wellness that are easy to forget: activity, meals and sleep.

Make patterns visible

Use progress views and recent history to understand consistency instead of chasing one perfect day.

Turn insight into action

The dashboard prioritizes a small next action so the product remains useful after the data is entered.

Engineering behind FitFlow

Authentication

Firebase Authentication supports email/password and Google Sign-In with shared profile and session handling.

User data

Firebase Realtime Database stores wellness records so dashboards and progress views can work from the user's own history.

Explainable analytics

Charts and daily recommendations are derived from recorded data rather than depending on a hidden generative model.

Responsive experience

The authenticated product uses a shared design system so tracking, review and account pages feel like one application.

A deliberate choice

No fake user counts. No fake testimonials.

FitFlow is presented honestly as a product project that can be used and evaluated. We do not invent adoption numbers, customer stories or contact details to make the project look bigger than it is.
